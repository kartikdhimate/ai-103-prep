# Exam Readiness Checklist

Decision on Day 21. Check each criterion with evidence (a file, a score, a log). Official exam facts to verify before booking: study guide <https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/credentials/certifications/resources/study-guides/ai-103>.

## Knowledge and scores

- [ ] Practice assessment taken; overall score at or above 75% and no domain under 70% (this plan's rule), or the weak domain was repaired and re-checked with the scenario drill.
- [ ] Scenario drill in [DOMAIN_REVIEW.md](DOMAIN_REVIEW.md): at least 16 of 20 correct on the second pass.
- [ ] Practical assessment score at or above 70 (see [PRACTICAL_ASSESSMENT.md](PRACTICAL_ASSESSMENT.md)); target 85.
- [ ] All 64 objectives in [TRACKER.md](../TRACKER.md) have confidence 3 or higher, or a named, short remediation plan for each exception (maximum 5 exceptions).
- [ ] Every item in [WEAK_AREAS.md](WEAK_AREAS.md) is `Fixed` or has a dated plan.

## Hands-on evidence

- [ ] Capstone demo script ran end to end ([TASKS.md](../capstone/TASKS.md)).
- [ ] Retrieval metrics, groundedness evaluation, agent evaluation and red-team report exist.
- [ ] You can explain from memory: keyless auth, tool policy, indirect injection controls, RAG failure taxonomy.

## Exam logistics

- [ ] Exam date booked or a dated extension plan written.
- [ ] Identification and name match; delivery method chosen (online proctored or test center); system test done.
- [ ] Policies read: duration, scoring, retake, breaks (links in [RESOURCES.md](../RESOURCES.md)).
- [ ] Exam sandbox tried at least once.

## Budget and housekeeping

- [ ] Total prep spend recorded in [COST_TRACKER.md](../COST_TRACKER.md).
- [ ] Azure resources deleted or intentionally kept.

## Readiness decision

| Outcome | Rule | Action |
|---|---|---|
| **Go** | All knowledge, hands-on and logistics boxes checked | Take the exam within 7 days to keep recall high |
| **Go with focus** | One knowledge box missed by a small margin | 2 evenings on the weak domain, then take the exam |
| **Extend** | Two or more knowledge boxes missed or practical under 70 | 5-7 day plan: repeat Day 19 and Day 20 style sessions on the weakest domains only; reschedule |
| **Pause** | Major life or schedule disruption | Keep notes, delete billable resources, restart with a 1-day recap |

## Last 24 hours
