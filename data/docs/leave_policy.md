# Leave Policy

Company: Contoso Field Services (CFS)
Document ID: leave_policy
Owner: Elena Markovic, Director of Human Resources
Effective date: January 1, 2026
Review date: December 15, 2026

1. Purpose and scope

This policy defines paid leave, sick leave, family leave, and leave approval procedures for employees of Contoso Field Services. It applies to all full-time and part-time employees, including field technicians, dispatchers, customer support representatives, and office personnel. Contractors must follow the terms of their individual agreements.

2. Annual leave entitlement

Full-time employees receive 25 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Employees joining after January 1 receive a prorated allowance based on their remaining employment period. Part-time employees receive a proportional entitlement based on contracted working hours.

Annual leave must be requested through the CFS People Portal at least 10 calendar days before the first requested day. The employee's direct manager must approve or reject the request within three business days. Requests exceeding five consecutive working days also require approval from the relevant department director.

Employees may carry over up to five unused annual leave days into the next calendar year. Carried-over days must be used by March 31 or they expire, unless HR grants a written exception.

3. Sick leave

Employees must notify their direct manager and the Dispatch Desk at dispatch@contoso.example no later than 8:00 a.m. local time on the first day of an absence affecting a scheduled field assignment.

CFS provides up to 10 paid sick days per calendar year under this fictional company policy. Absences lasting more than three consecutive calendar days require a medical certificate submitted to HR within five business days of the employee's return.

Employees must not disclose detailed medical diagnoses to dispatchers or customers. HR maintains medical documentation separately from operational records.

4. Family and emergency leave

Employees may take three paid working days for the death of an immediate family member. Immediate family includes a spouse, domestic partner, parent, child, or sibling.

Managers may authorize up to two paid emergency leave days per calendar year for urgent personal circumstances. Additional leave requires written approval from Elena Markovic.

5. Responsibilities and records

Managers must ensure field coverage before approving leave. The Dispatch Desk maintains the operational absence calendar, while HR maintains official leave balances.

Employees can appeal a rejected leave request to Elena Markovic within five business days. HR will issue a written decision within three business days of receiving the appeal.

Questions about this policy should be sent to hr@contoso.example.
