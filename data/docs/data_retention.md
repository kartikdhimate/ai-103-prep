# Data Retention Policy

Company: Contoso Field Services (CFS)
Document ID: data_retention
Owner: Sofia Lindholm, Data Protection Officer
Effective date: January 1, 2026
Review date: December 15, 2026

1. Purpose and scope

This policy establishes how Contoso Field Services retains, archives, and deletes company information. It applies to electronic records, paper files, customer service tickets, personnel records, financial documents, and field service reports.

Employees must store business records in approved company systems. Saving confidential company information in personal email accounts or unapproved consumer cloud services is prohibited.

2. Retention periods

The following retention periods apply unless a legal requirement, contractual obligation, or documented legal hold requires a different period.

Record category	Retention period	Responsible owner
Customer service tickets	3 years after closure	Service Operations
Field maintenance reports	5 years after completion	Field Operations
Expense claims and receipts	7 years after the financial year ends	Finance
Employee personnel records	7 years after employment ends	Human Resources
Security incident records	5 years after incident closure	Information Security
Routine system access logs	12 months after creation	IT Operations
Approved equipment loan records	2 years after equipment return	IT Asset Management

Where applicable law requires a longer period, the longer legally required period takes precedence.

3. Storage and access

Records must be stored in approved systems with role-based access controls. Department managers are responsible for reviewing access permissions at least every six months.

Sensitive personnel records and security incident records may be accessed only by authorized personnel with a legitimate business need. Employees must not download complete customer databases onto local devices unless the Data Protection Officer has approved the activity in writing.

Backups are maintained for 90 days. Backup retention does not replace the retention periods specified above. Restored backups must not reintroduce records that were properly deleted, where technically feasible.

4. Deletion and legal holds

When a retention period expires, the record owner must initiate deletion or anonymization within 30 calendar days. IT Operations must complete approved deletion requests within 10 business days after receiving an authorized request.

No employee may delete records subject to an active investigation, litigation hold, regulatory inquiry, or preservation instruction.

Legal holds are issued in writing by the Legal Department. Sofia Lindholm must maintain a register of active holds and review that register every 90 days. Records covered by a hold may be deleted only after written release from Legal.

5. Responsibilities and exceptions

Department managers must identify record owners and ensure retention schedules are followed. Employees must report accidental disclosure, unauthorized copying, or suspected improper deletion to Information Security.

Exceptions require written approval from Sofia Lindholm and the relevant department director. Approvals must state the record category, reason, and revised retention period.
