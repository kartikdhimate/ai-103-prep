# Employee Onboarding

Company: Contoso Field Services (CFS)
Document ID: onboarding
Owner: Hannah Virtanen, People Operations Manager
Effective date: January 1, 2026
Review date: December 15, 2026

1. Purpose and scope

This document defines the onboarding process for new employees joining Contoso Field Services. It applies to permanent employees, fixed-term employees, and employees transferring into a new operational role. Contractors follow the same security orientation requirements but may have different employment administration procedures.

The hiring manager is accountable for ensuring the employee completes all mandatory onboarding activities.

2. Before the first working day

The hiring manager must submit a completed onboarding request to People Operations at least five business days before the employee's start date.

The request must include the employee's legal name, job title, department, manager, start date, work location, and required system access.

People Operations must issue the employment documentation and collect signed acknowledgments before the employee begins productive work. IT Operations must prepare the assigned laptop, multifactor authentication method, and required applications by 16:00 on the last business day before the start date.

The employee's manager must assign a workplace mentor no later than two business days before the start date.

3. First-day requirements

On the first working day, employees must attend a 90-minute orientation session led by Hannah Virtanen or a designated People Operations representative.

The session covers company structure, workplace conduct, leave procedures, expense claims, and reporting channels. Employees must complete the information security introduction and acknowledge the Acceptable Use Policy before receiving access to customer production systems.

Field employees must also complete a 60-minute field safety briefing before their first customer-site assignment.

The employee's manager must confirm completion of the first-day checklist in the CFS People Portal by 17:00 local time.

4. First-week activities

By the end of the fifth working day, the employee must complete:

Information security and phishing awareness training.
Data handling and customer confidentiality training.
An introduction to the Service Desk and incident reporting process.
Role-specific systems training.
A review of performance objectives with the manager.

The manager must schedule a 30-minute check-in on the third working day and a 45-minute review on the tenth working day.

5. First 30 and 90 days

Within 30 calendar days of joining, the employee must complete all assigned mandatory training. Employees with outstanding mandatory training may lose access to affected systems until completion.

At day 30, the manager documents progress against the employee's initial objectives. At day 90, the manager and employee conduct a formal onboarding review covering performance, training, equipment, and support needs.

Hannah Virtanen reviews onboarding completion statistics monthly. Any employee missing a mandatory requirement after 30 days must receive a documented remediation plan within five business days.
