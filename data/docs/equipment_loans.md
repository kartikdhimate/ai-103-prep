# Equipment Loans

Company: Contoso Field Services (CFS)
Document ID: equipment_loans
Owner: Oliver Reed, IT Asset Manager
Effective date: January 1, 2026
Review date: December 15, 2026

1. Purpose and scope

This policy governs the temporary assignment and return of company-owned equipment at Contoso Field Services. Covered items include laptops, mobile phones, tablets, diagnostic devices, portable network equipment, and specialist field tools.

Equipment remains the property of CFS at all times. Employees may use assigned equipment only for authorized business purposes.

2. Request and approval

Employees must submit an Equipment Loan Request through the CFS IT Portal. The request must include the employee's name, department, asset type, business purpose, collection date, expected return date, and manager approval.

Standard laptops and mobile phones require approval from the employee's direct manager and IT Asset Management. Specialist diagnostic equipment additionally requires approval from the Field Operations Manager.

Requests for equipment valued above €1,500 require approval from both Oliver Reed and the relevant department director.

IT Asset Management must acknowledge complete requests within two business days. Standard equipment should be issued within five business days of approval, subject to availability.

3. Loan periods and inventory

Standard laptop and mobile phone loans may last up to 90 calendar days before renewal is required. Specialist equipment loans may last up to 30 calendar days unless Field Operations approves an extension.

Every issued item must have a unique asset tag recorded in the CFS Asset Register. The register must include the asset tag, serial number, assigned employee, issue date, condition, accessories, and scheduled return date.

Employees must sign an electronic receipt when collecting equipment. IT Asset Management conducts a monthly overdue-loan review on the first business day of each month.

4. Security and care

Employees must protect loaned equipment from theft, unauthorized access, and physical damage. Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and multifactor authentication where supported.

Employees must not install unapproved software, disable endpoint protection, lend devices to family members, or allow another employee to use their assigned credentials.

Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to the Security Incident team within 15 minutes of discovery whenever reasonably possible. Damage must be reported within one business day.

5. Return and renewal

Employees must return equipment by the recorded due date or submit a renewal request at least three business days beforehand.

Returned equipment must include all accessories listed on the electronic receipt. IT Asset Management must inspect returned items within two business days and update the Asset Register within one business day after inspection.

When employment ends, all assigned equipment must be returned by the employee's final working day unless HR and IT approve a different arrangement in writing.

Disputes concerning equipment condition or missing accessories must be submitted to Oliver Reed within five business days.
