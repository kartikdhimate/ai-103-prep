# Business Travel Policy

Company: Contoso Field Services (CFS)
Document ID: travel
Owner: Laura Bennett, Travel and Procurement Manager
Effective date: January 1, 2026
Review date: December 15, 2026

1. Purpose and scope

This policy governs business travel undertaken by Contoso Field Services employees. It applies to customer visits, field assignments, conferences, training, and internal business meetings outside the employee's normal work location.

Employees must use the approved CFS Travel Portal for reservations and expense reporting.

2. Authorization

Domestic travel within Finland requires written approval from the employee's direct manager at least three business days before departure.

International travel requires approval from both the department director and Laura Bennett at least seven business days before departure. Travel involving a destination classified as high risk by the company's travel risk provider requires additional written approval from the Chief Operating Officer.

Travel requests must state the business purpose, destination, departure and return dates, estimated cost, customer or event, and any special accessibility requirements.

Employees must not make nonrefundable bookings before the required approvals are recorded.

3. Transportation and accommodation

Economy-class airfare is the default for flights. Rail travel is preferred for journeys where the scheduled rail trip is six hours or less and a suitable connection is available.

Employees should book domestic transport at least seven calendar days in advance and international flights at least 14 calendar days in advance when operational requirements permit.

Hotel accommodation is limited to €160 per night in Finland and €220 per night internationally, excluding taxes. Exceptions require prior written approval from Laura Bennett.

Rental cars may be used when public transport is impractical or when transporting specialist equipment. Compact or midsize vehicles are the default categories. Luxury vehicle upgrades are not reimbursable.

4. Travel safety and changes

Employees must register international itineraries and emergency contact details in the Travel Portal before departure. The traveler must keep the emergency hotline, +358 9 5550 1299, accessible during the trip.

Employees must report a serious travel-related safety incident to their manager and the emergency hotline as soon as safely possible. Employees must not continue travel into a newly designated high-risk area without written clearance from the Chief Operating Officer.

Changes caused by customer emergencies may be approved by the department director after booking. The reason and approval must be recorded in the Travel Portal within two business days.

5. Cancellations and reimbursement

Employees must cancel unused reservations promptly to reduce avoidable costs. Cancellation fees are reimbursable when the cancellation is business-related and documented.

Travel expenses must be submitted within 30 calendar days after the trip ends. Receipts are required for expenses of €10 or more. The expense limits policy governs meals, mileage, parking, and incidental purchases.

Laura Bennett reviews travel spending monthly and reports exceptions to Finance by the tenth business day of the following month.
