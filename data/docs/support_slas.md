# Support Service-Level Agreements (SLAs)

Company: Contoso Field Services (CFS)
Document ID: support_slas
Owner: Priya Nair, Head of Service Operations
Effective date: January 1, 2026
Review date: December 15, 2026

1. Purpose and coverage

This policy defines response and restoration targets for internal support services provided by Contoso Field Services. The Service Desk operates Monday through Friday, from 07:00 to 19:00 Eastern European Time (EET/EEST as applicable), excluding company holidays. The emergency hotline operates 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

Tickets must be submitted through the CFS Support Portal or by calling the Service Desk at +358 9 5550 1200.

2. Incident priorities

Support tickets are assigned one of four priorities.

Priority	Definition	Initial response target	Restoration target
P1 — Critical	A major outage affecting multiple customer sites or a suspected active security breach	15 minutes	4 hours
P2 — High	A critical business function is unavailable at one customer site	30 minutes	8 business hours
P3 — Normal	A noncritical application or device is impaired, but work can continue	4 business hours	3 business days
P4 — Low	A minor request, cosmetic defect, or general information request	1 business day	5 business days

Response time begins when a ticket is recorded in the support system. Restoration time begins at the same point and measures the time until service is restored or an approved workaround is implemented.

Targets are service objectives, not guarantees of permanent resolution. Scheduled maintenance and delays caused by customer access restrictions must be documented in the ticket.

3. Escalation procedure

P1 incidents must be escalated immediately to the on-call Service Operations Manager. If no acknowledgment is received within 10 minutes, the technician must call the emergency hotline at +358 9 5550 1299.

P2 incidents must be escalated to the team lead if service is not restored within four business hours. P3 incidents must be escalated after two business days without meaningful progress.

Priya Nair receives an automated notification for every P1 incident and any P2 incident that misses its restoration target.

4. Communication requirements

For P1 incidents, the incident owner must provide customer updates at least every 30 minutes. For P2 incidents, updates are required every two business hours. P3 and P4 customers receive updates at least once per business day while the ticket remains active.

All customer communications and escalation decisions must be recorded in the support ticket.

5. Reporting and review

The Service Desk publishes a monthly SLA report by the fifth business day of the following month. Reports include response compliance, restoration compliance, reopened tickets, and priority distribution.

Priya Nair reviews missed targets monthly. Repeated failures require a corrective action plan within 10 business days.
