# Expense Limits

Company: Contoso Field Services (CFS)
Document ID: expense_limits
Owner: Marcus Chen, Finance Controller
Effective date: January 1, 2026
Review date: December 15, 2026

1. Purpose and scope

This policy establishes spending limits and reimbursement requirements for employees of Contoso Field Services. It applies to business travel, customer visits, field maintenance, approved training, and other authorized company activities. Personal purchases are not reimbursable.

2. Meal and accommodation limits

Employees traveling on approved business may claim meals up to the following amounts:

Breakfast: €15 per person.
Lunch: €20 per person.
Dinner: €35 per person.
Daily meal maximum: €70 per person.

The daily maximum takes precedence over the individual meal limits. Alcohol is never reimbursable.

Hotel accommodation is limited to €160 per night, excluding taxes, when the employee travels within Finland. International accommodation is limited to €220 per night, excluding taxes. Any exception requires written approval from Marcus Chen before booking.

3. Transportation

Standard-class rail tickets and economy-class airfare are reimbursable for approved business travel. Employees should book rail travel at least seven calendar days in advance and air travel at least 14 calendar days in advance when schedules permit.

Personal vehicle mileage is reimbursed at €0.45 per kilometer. Employees must record the date, destination, business purpose, and distance traveled. Parking is reimbursable up to €25 per day, and tolls are reimbursable at actual cost.

Taxi or ride-hailing expenses are limited to €60 per journey. A higher amount requires prior written approval from the employee's department director. Commuting between home and the employee's normal workplace is not reimbursable.

4. Equipment and incidental purchases

Field supplies costing €100 or less per item may be purchased with manager approval. Purchases exceeding €100 require approval from both the manager and Finance. Any single purchase above €1,000 also requires approval from Marcus Chen, regardless of department budget.

Subscriptions, software licenses, and personal electronic devices cannot be charged to expenses without written authorization from the IT Director.

5. Submission and reimbursement

Employees must submit expense claims through the CFS Finance Portal within 30 calendar days of the transaction. Receipts are required for all expenses of €10 or more. Claims below €10 must still identify the date, vendor, amount, and business purpose.

Managers must review claims within five business days. Finance processes approved claims within 10 business days of final approval.

Fraudulent claims may result in disciplinary action. Employees may appeal a rejected claim to Marcus Chen within five business days of receiving the rejection.
